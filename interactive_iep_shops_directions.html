IEP support: use the picture clues, reduce choices to two, or have an adult read the options aloud.

2. Listening: Missing direction words

Teacher/adult reads each sentence aloud. The student chooses the missing word.

a. ______ can I get to the café?
b. ______ is the best way to the café?
c. ______ is the café?
d. Do you know ______ the café is?

Adult prompt: say the sentence once, pause, then repeat slowly if needed. Accept pointing to the answer.
